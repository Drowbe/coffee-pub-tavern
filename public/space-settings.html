Space

Decides which image sections show up for this space on a member's own profile page, and which ones Coffee Pub Studio offers to publish for it.

Link icon

Shown on the button next to Join, and in the call toolbar, once a launch link is set above.

Members

Click a player to add or remove them from this space -- saves as you click.

Invite to register

A link that signs someone up with a real account and drops them straight into this space (and the Lobby, like everyone). Works even while general sign-up is off. Single use, expires after 7 days.

Guest link

Anyone with this link joins this space with just a name -- no account, no invite needed. Anyone already in the space can also manage this from the space itself, not just here.

Modules

Modules with a space panel. Tick one to make it available to this space's members, in the Modules button on the call toolbar. A module marked on for every space is set on the Modules tab of Manage.

AI

Modules in this space can use the server's AI service to summarise, answer questions and write cards from what a person selects. Turn it off here to stop that in this space, whatever the roles allow.

What each module does in this space. Space moderators can change these too.
