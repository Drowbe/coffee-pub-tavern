Reactions

The emoji tray at the table, and on stream. The first six are also reachable with keys 1 to 6. Leave the list empty to turn reactions off.

Font Awesome

Icons you want available. On fontawesome.com, copy an icon's HTML (like <i class="fa-solid fa-dice"></i>) and paste it here; the preview shows what it draws. Only the Free icons that ship with Tavern will show.

Rooms

Everyone belongs to the Lobby. Each room is its own conversation for the users you pick; players see their rooms after signing in and choose one. Coffee Pub Studio shows one room at a time.

Roles

What each kind of person can do. Admin can do everything and can't be turned down. Moderator is what someone gets in any room where they're marked Moderator on their profile's Rooms tab. Guest is everyone who joins from a room's guest link. Changes save as you click.

Kicking, muting, guest links and asides are enforced by the server. Chat, pictures, reactions and screen sharing are enforced by the page itself (they travel between browsers, not through the server), so they hold for honest use, not for someone determined to get around them.

Modules

Add features by uploading a module zip. A module runs in the browser, in a sandbox, and only reaches Tavern through what it asks for below. Upload it, look over what it asks for, then turn it on.

    Troubleshooting

    Layout diagnostics -- shows what this device and browser report about the screen: viewport heights, safe areas and where fixed bars land. Open it in the browser and from the home screen app, and send screenshots when the layout is wrong on a phone.

    Participant video defaults

    Borders are drawn around the OBS boxes while the participant talks or while their microphone is off. Untick one to never draw it. They are the same for everyone.

    Participant borders
    Character borders

    While the Participant box shows a picture instead of the camera (offline, or camera off), it can sit on a colour so the video area stays visible, and smaller than the box to leave a margin around it. The camera always fills the box.

    A member's Participant and Character boxes dim and/or tint like this in OBS whenever they aren't actually here right now: fully offline, online but pulled into an aside while the stream is following someone else, or in a Private Conversation specifically (which also always forces their live video off, on top of whatever's set here). The two people actually in an ordinary aside stay untouched. Dim and Tint are independent -- Dim darkens the whole box, Tint lays a colour over it at its own opacity -- use either, both, or neither.

    OBS access

    OBS never signs in. Each user's OBS view link carries this stream key instead of a login, and Coffee Pub Studio fetches it by itself when you sign in there. You only need it here to build a view link by hand. Regenerating it makes every existing link stop working; Studio re-points its sources on its next sync.
