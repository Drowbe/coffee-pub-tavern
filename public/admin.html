Player video defaults

Borders are drawn around the OBS boxes while the player talks or while their microphone is off. Untick one to never draw it. They are the same for everyone.

Player borders
Character borders

While the Player box shows a picture instead of the camera (offline, or camera off), it can sit on a colour so the video area stays visible, and smaller than the box to leave a margin around it. The camera always fills the box.

OBS access

OBS never signs in. Each user's OBS view link carries this stream key instead of a login, and Coffee Pub Studio fetches it by itself when you sign in there. You only need it here to build a view link by hand. Regenerating it makes every existing link stop working; Studio re-points its sources on its next sync.
