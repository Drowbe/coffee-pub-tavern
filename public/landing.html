What it is

A call that stays open

Video and voice for the whole group, with a chat beside it. Step into a side conversation and come back; pop the call out into its own window; join with only the chat when that is all you need.

Tools beside the call

Open the calendar next to the video, float the planner over it, snap the to-do list into a grid. Every tool is a pane you can dock, float or open in its own window, and your layout is remembered.

Tools that know each other

Drag a task onto a day. Drop a place on the plan. Link a poll to a decision and let its result land where it belongs. Nothing is copied; everything points at the one real thing.

The modules

Turn on the ones your group needs. Each environment picks its own.

  • PlannerA trip day by day, or anything that unfolds over dates: what you know, what your options are, what you have decided. Flights, stays, stops, ideas between days, costs and who paid.
  • CalendarThe group's dates, a month, a week or a list, with the events of every space you belong to.
  • To-doTasks with due dates, linked to the things they are about, with reminders for the people who are away.
  • PollsDecide together: pick one, pick any, suggest another option; the result can carry straight onto a plan.
  • PlacesThe places that matter, yours and the group's, with an address, a position and a note.
  • MapsEverything with a position on one map, including regions kept on the server for when you are offline.
  • ResearchNotes, links, photos and answers, tagged and shared, laid out as cards or a list.
  • AssistantAsk about anything the group holds, and drop the answer where it is useful.

How it works

  1. You get an environment. Its own address, its own sign-in, its own people. Nothing in it is shared with anyone else's.
  2. You make spaces in it. A space is a table: a call, a chat and the modules you turn on for it. A family might have one; a company might have one per team; a game night has one per campaign.
  3. People join with a link. Members sign in; guests can join a space with just a name when you allow it.
  4. You run it, we host it. The owner sets the name, the look, the members, the spaces and the modules. The rest -- the servers, the call service, the updates, the backups -- is ours. Or run the whole thing yourself: the same software is available to self-host.

Plans
